🧬 Immunogenomics – HLA Typing Analysis

Objective:

To analyze Human Leukocyte Antigen (HLA) alleles and understand immunogenetic variations associated with disease.

🔬 Workflow

Processed sequencing data for HLA analysis
Performed HLA allele prediction using OptiType
Identified class I HLA alleles (HLA-A, HLA-B, HLA-C)
Interpreted immunogenetic variation in the context of disease

🛠 Tools Used

OptiType
Python-based pipeline (for execution and preprocessing)

📊 Outputs

Predicted HLA alleles
Immunogenetic interpretation of results

💡 Key Insight

HLA molecules play a crucial role in antigen presentation
Variations in HLA alleles are associated with immune response and autoimmune diseases
This analysis helps understand the genetic basis of immune-related conditions

🔗 Relevance to Multi-Omics

HLA typing adds an immunogenomics layer to the multi-omics framework, complementing transcriptomics, epigenomics, and proteomics for a more comprehensive understanding of disease mechanisms.
